About the Hotel

More than a place to sleep — a place to feel welcome.

Constant Hotel & Suites is a welcoming destination in Ogidi for guests seeking comfortable accommodation, relaxed evenings and enjoyable social experiences. Whether you are in town for a short stay, visiting family, or gathering with old friends, you will find a warm atmosphere, convenient service and friendly hospitality.

From quiet rooms to unwind in, to a lively bar where the big match is always an event, Constant Hotel & Suites is built around what matters most to our guests: comfort, convenience, and good company. Residents and visitors alike are welcome.

  • Comfortable accommodation for leisure and business stays
  • A social bar for football, drinks and good company
  • A natural meeting point for December homecomings and hangouts

The Local Kitchen

Home-Style Igbo Dishes, Made With Love.

Craving the taste of home? From ukwa and ofe onugbu to catfish pepper soup, enjoy familiar local flavours during your stay — a favourite part of every visit, especially at Christmas.

Ukwa

A treasured Igbo classic — breadfruit porridge with a rich, nutty taste, best enjoyed the traditional way.

Ofe Onugbu

Rich bitterleaf soup, slow-cooked the way home should taste — perfect with your favourite swallow.

Catfish Pepper Soup

Warm, peppery and restorative — a soothing bowl for cool evenings and late-night cravings.

Nkwobi

Spiced cow-foot delicacy in a rich palm-oil sauce — a true table favourite, made for sharing with friends.

Abacha

African salad — fresh, tangy and satisfying, dressed up with all the works.

Okpa

Hearty, golden and steamed to perfection — simple, filling and proudly Igbo.
